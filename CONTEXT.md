# Workshop glossary

- Cartoon: a complete directed performance on one scenic backdrop.
- Actor slot: one of two independently directed performers; a prop also occupies a slot.
- Action: a reusable animated performance such as a walk or wave.
- Cue: the frame at which an actor starts an action at a placement and motion.
- Frame: one twelfth of a second of authored cartoon time.
- Film: commit a cue at the current frame, replacing that slot's cues from that frame onward.
- Playhead: the frame being inspected or performed.
- Take: the current working cartoon.
